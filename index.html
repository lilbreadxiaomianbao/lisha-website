About Lisha

About me.

I'm a licensed Co-operators advisor serving every corner of Ontario — Toronto, the GTA, Ottawa, Waterloo, Windsor, and every small town in between. My work starts with listening: to a family's routines, a business's real risks, a newcomer's quiet worries. Only then does it become insurance.

Co-operators has been Canadian-owned and co-operatively run since 1945. That heritage shapes how I advise: patient, transparent, and never pressured. When claims happen — and one day they will — you'll know your advisor's name, not a call-centre extension.

Because insurance is personal, I work with clients in the language they think in — English, Mandarin, or Cantonese. Clarity in your mother tongue isn't a nicety. It's the whole point.

Why Co-operators

Why Co-operators.

Co-operators is one of the few Canadian insurers still structured as a co-operative. That's not a marketing line — it changes how decisions are made, how claims are paid, and how premiums are set.

  • i.

    Canadian-owned since 1945

    Headquartered in Guelph, Ontario. Never sold, never outsourced to a foreign owner. Your premiums stay in Canada.

  • ii.

    Community Guard®

    A coverage line built specifically for community organizations, non-profits, and co-operatives. Unique to Co-operators — no other Canadian insurer offers it.

  • iii.

    Values over shareholders

    Co-operative governance means long-term client outcomes outweigh quarterly returns. Decisions are made with clients, not around them.

  • iv.

    Certified B Corp

    Sustainability and social responsibility written into the governance structure, with measurable commitments to climate and community resilience.
